# v3.0 as-built checks

The 2023 Caltronics run was assembled from `Production/Margay_BoM_3v0_-_2023-03-17.csv` (the copy in `CaltronicsQuote_2023-08-01.zip`). In three places that BoM disagreed with the design, so what is on the boards is not known from the files:

| Ref | Design | 2023 BoM | Net |
|-----|--------|----------|-----|
| R7, R9 | 1 kΩ, 0402 | DIGIKEY 1 kΩ 0402 (`311-1.00KLRCT-ND`); MPN 330 Ω 0603 (`RC0603FR-07330RL`) | UART series resistors: ATmega1284P RX/TX to FT231X |
| R19 | 100 kΩ, 0402 | 100 Ω, both DIGIKEY and MPN (`311-100LRCT-ND`, `RC0402FR-07100RL`) | 3V3 to Q2 gate (`N$6`); Q3 grounds `N$6` while USB is connected |
| U13 | MAX6070 | MAX6071 (#44, confirmed by Bobby Schulz) | 1.8 V reference |

Measure two or three boards from the run, so that one odd board does not stand for all 50.

All four parts are on the top side. The images come from `Hardware/Margay.brd` (pads, silkscreen, and outline only); the reference designators shown are not printed on the boards.

## 1. Look (unpowered)

1. R7 and R9: 0402 (1.0 × 0.5 mm) or 0603 (1.6 × 0.8 mm)? A 0603 on these pads overhangs them.
2. U13: record the top marking.

## 2. Resistance (unpowered, batteries and USB out)

R7 and R9 sit between two unpowered ICs, whose protection diodes can conduct at an ohmmeter's test voltage. Take each reading in both probe polarities and record the higher one.

| Ref | Probe across | Expect if correct | Expect if 2023 MPN |
|-----|--------------|-------------------|--------------------|
| R7 | its two pads | 1 kΩ | 330 Ω |
| R9 | its two pads | 1 kΩ | 330 Ω |
| R19 | its two pads; red on the Q2-gate pad (away from the USB-C connector), black on the 3V3 pad (toward it) | 100 kΩ | 100 Ω |

For R19, the red-on-gate polarity keeps Q3's body diode reverse-biased, so the reading is R19 alone.

## 3. R19 under USB (powered)

With the board on USB and running, measure the voltage across R19. The current is that voltage divided by R19 from step 2: about 33 mA at 100 Ω, about 33 µA at 100 kΩ.

**Reading (2026-11-04):** R7 and R9 are 330 Ω, the value of the BoM's manufacturer number (`RC0603FR-07330RL`), not its DigiKey number (1 kΩ). R19 is 100 Ω, as both BoM columns said. R7 was 330 Ω by design until Bobby Schulz's `904d985` (2019-07-19) changed R7 and R9 to 1 kΩ and left the 330 Ω manufacturer number in place, so builds since then likely carry 330 Ω as well.
